## 🌟 About

**Proxy Browser Extension** is a lightweight, privacy-first browser extension that lets you **route your traffic through servers in 30+ countries** with a single click. No accounts, no logs, no nonsense — just clean, fast, encrypted browsing.

Perfect for accessing geo-restricted content, protecting your identity on public Wi-Fi, or simply keeping your browsing habits private from ISPs and trackers.
